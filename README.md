# Assignment-1-NALI2026
🎓 My NALI 2026 Learning Experience | UTM

I’m pleased to share a meaningful learning experience from New Academia Learning Innovation (NALI) 2026, held at Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) on 28–29 October 2026.

As part of our Technology and Information Systems (SECP1513) assignment, I was responsible for conducting an interview related to the SeaClear project.

🌊 Exploring the SeaClear Innovation

The project, titled “SeaClear – Transforming Bathymetric Data Processing into Authentic Hydrographic Learning,” explores how real-world bathymetric data can be transformed into a practical and meaningful learning experience.

Through the poster and interview with Novajira Bin Muhammad Fadil, a Master’s student, I learned how SeaClear uses a MATLAB-based filtering system to help students:

* Explore real-world bathymetric survey data
* Identify sea-spike anomalies
* Compare different filtering strategies
* Develop critical thinking and professional judgement

💡 What I Learned

What interested me most was how SeaClear connects technical knowledge with real-world applications. It showed me that ICT is not only about learning programming or understanding technical concepts, but also about using technology to solve practical problems and support meaningful learning.

Being involved in the interview also allowed me to practise preparing questions, communicating with a presenter, and learning about an innovation beyond what was presented on the poster.

This experience broadened my understanding of educational technology and encouraged me to explore how ICT can support learning beyond the classroom.

Key Takeaway: Technology becomes more meaningful when students can connect what they learn with real-world applications.

Grateful for this opportunity to learn, explore, and gain new perspectives! ✨
